Fraud Emergency Guidance

If you have shared financial credentials or transferred money to an unauthorized recipient, execute these steps immediately.

NATIONAL CYBERCRIME HELPLINE 1930

Report financial cyber fraud immediately to trigger the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) banking freeze protocol.

Immediate Response Checklist

1
Contact Your Bank / UPI Provider

Call your bank's official toll-free customer care immediately to block debit cards, freeze net-banking, and revoke UPI registration.

2
File on National Cybercrime Portal

Submit transaction IDs, timestamps, and scammer details on official portal: cybercrime.gov.in.

3
Report Suspected Communication on Chakshu

Report the fraudulent mobile number, WhatsApp handle, or SMS sender on the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) Chakshu facility: sancharsaathi.gov.in.

4
Secure Your Mobile Device & SIM

If you downloaded any remote-support app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) or APK, turn off Wi-Fi/Mobile Data immediately and uninstall the app.

Critical Rules for Victims
  • Never share OTPs or PINs with anyone claiming to help or "reverse" transactions.
  • Beware of fake recovery agents on social media or search engines claiming they can hack or recover lost funds for an upfront fee.
  • ScamShield AI provides defensive intelligence only and cannot directly reverse bank transactions or recover funds.
